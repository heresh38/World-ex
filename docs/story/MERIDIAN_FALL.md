## The reason
Meridian was one of Earth's great cities, the old capital. It was not taken: it was hit. When the ring broke, one
of its stations came down on the city, and the Sarketh landing force came down after it. They made their
stronghold where the station's heart lies, at the top of the city: the Crown. They have held Meridian ever since.

Three years ago an Institute team went into the research annex on the canal to strip what was left of the station's
systems, and brought its deep-space array back to life to read it. They wanted to take it apart. They gave the
Sarketh a transmitter.

Eleven nights ago the beam lit. The Sarketh are not calling for a way home. They are calling their own people to
come and take the system. The Institute's measure is that in about nineteen nights the beam will be strong enough
to reach the relay left at the edge of the system. Nobody knows whether anything will answer. Nobody will wait to
find out. The field round the beam makes a storm that takes anything that flies, so a small team walks in from the
city's edge.

Reach the Crown. Destroy the three hearts under the station that feed the beam. Kill the commander in his Colossus.
Bring out the Sarketh **command core**: everything their landing force knew, where the other species hold ground in
Sol among it. Its last order is given in the name of a throne that had already fallen: the first hint of what
happened to the empire.

Nothing in the level has to be rebuilt for this (it already stands under a broken ring, with the Crown at its top).
Only what it says changes (the tagline, the four briefing lines, the twenty radio lines, the end screen), and its
enemies' bodies and names with the redesign.

## Why the player, why on foot, why now
- **Now**: the beam needs about nineteen more nights to reach the relay at the edge of the system.
- **On foot**: the field round the beam makes a storm that takes anything that flies. The Directorate tried a
  strike nine nights ago; the gunship is the wreck across the Causeway. The landing pad at Ashfall Verge is as far
  as anything flies.
- **A small team**: a column would bring the whole garrison down on it. Three can get under it.
- **What is in it for us**: the call stops, and the Sarketh command core comes home: where every other species
  holds ground in Sol, and what the Sarketh were last told.
- **What is in it for the player**: the reward sets are made from what the team brings out. The Void Requiem guns
  and the Void Sentinel armour are cut from the Crown's own crystal, which is why they are earned here.
- **Why the Sarketh are here and do not leave**: this is where they landed, the wreck's heart powers them, and they
  mean to have the system. Their officers hold every gate, their works under the station feed the beam, and their
  commander has not left the Crown.
