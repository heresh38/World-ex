(`BRIEFING`, `INTEL`, `OUTCOME`), written into `Data/op_meridian.json`.

## The reason, in four sentences
Eleven nights ago a beam lit over Meridian, the old capital, silent since the Stillfall. Every night it burns, the
Keystones in Nexus Prime's wall lose ground: two districts are already sealed, and the Directorate gives the wall
about nineteen nights. Nothing that flies survives the storm over the city (the last gunship lies across the
Causeway), so a small team walks in from the edge. Reach the Crown, kill what feeds the beam and what guards it, and
bring the capital's Keystone home.

## What is really going on (learned on the way, never said at the start)
- Meridian was built on a **root**. Its centre is the Crown; the Concord Ring, the terraces and the boulevards are
  the root's rings with streets laid over them. Nobody who lived there knew.
- The **Keystones** in Nexus Prime's wall were not made by people. They were cut from the stone at the middle of
  Meridian's root when the wall was raised. The city's field is a splinter of the thing it keeps out.
- Three years ago a Pale Institute team set up in the research annex on the canal to listen to the root. To hear it
  better they coupled it to the capital's old orbital uplink on the Crown. They wanted to listen. They taught it to
  speak. Nobody came back.
- The beam is the root **calling its pieces home**. It is not a weapon and it is not aimed at the city out of malice:
  the splinters in the wall are answering, and every night they answer a little more the Quiet thins.
- So for the Network this place is two things: its **voice** and its **wound**. That is why its heaviest forms are
  here and why they do not leave: Bulwarks at every gate, a hive under the station pumping into the old traction
  mains to feed the beam, and under the Crown the **Hollow Colossus**, a guardian that slept there longer than the
  city stood and woke when the beam lit.
- The **Still Choir** came to hear it speak. Their banners hang on the terraces. Most of them are still here, and
  some of what the team fights on the way up wears their red.

## Why the player, why on foot, why now
- **Now**: the wall has nights, not months.
- **On foot**: the storm over the city takes aircraft, and the beam ruins guidance. The Directorate tried a strike
  nine nights ago; the gunship is the wreck across the Causeway. The landing pad at Ashfall Verge is as far as
  anything flies.
- **A small team**: a column would wake everything between the Verge and the Crown. Three can get under it.
- **What is in it for the city**: the beam goes dark (the pull on the wall stops), and the capital's Keystone, the
  stone the others were cut from, comes home. With it the wall's field can be rebuilt instead of patched.
- **What is in it for the player**: the reward sets are made from what the team brings out. The Void Requiem guns
  and the Void Sentinel armour are cut from the Crown's own crystal, which is why they are earned here and nowhere
  else.
- **What it costs**: the thing the city is about to bolt into its wall is the heart of the thing it fears. The
  operation ends on that note and the campaign picks it up.
