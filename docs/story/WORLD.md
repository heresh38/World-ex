# Shattered Orbit: the world, in our words

The owner adopted the story bible in `art/reference/story` (screenshots of a concept document) on 2026-11-09: its
world, factions, enemies and campaign, with Nexus Prime as the walled city and every term that echoes another game
renamed. This file is the text version the game is written against. Where the bible is silent, the entry says who
filled it in. Names marked (proposed) are ours and can be changed in one place.

## What happened
- **The Stillfall** (2189). No explosion, no fleet. For eleven minutes every clock on Earth stopped and people heard
  something enormous breathing under the ground. Afterwards the planet was different: forests where cities stood,
  mountains split open on structures of unfamiliar material, coasts moved, new organisms out of the soil. It never
  actually ended.
- **The Pale Network**. Under every continent lies the Deep Architecture: structures that grow, rearrange themselves
  and answer living things, each built as rings round a dark middle. The oldest records call them **roots**. It is one
  planetary organism. It does not think like a person; it treats Earth as one living system and is folding everything
  into it. The Stillfall was the day it became active enough to change the whole planet. Nobody in the world knows
  this; the player pieces it together.
- **The Altered**. Not one species: the Network's responses, each for a job. Stalkers hunt, Bulwarks guard nests and
  roots, Veilborn hide a place until you cross a line, Colossi move through whole regions, Remnants repeat pieces of
  human life (voices, habits, the outline of a settlement). Some carry human patterns. In our game they also carry
  our guns: the Remnants kept what they held when they changed.
- **Shattered Orbit** (ours: the bible says nothing about space). Humanity had a ring of stations round the planet and
  a ground station under it in the capital. In the eleven minutes the ring broke. Its wreck still hangs over Meridian.

## The city and why it sends people out
- **Nexus Prime**, the last walled city (the bible's Vesper). Millions, built upward against the wall. It lives on
  salvage, rationed farming and expeditions.
- **The Keystones** (proposed; the bible's "Wardens", a word our game already uses for a creature). Devices set in the
  wall. They hold a field, **the Quiet** (proposed), that keeps the Stillfall's changes out of the city. The field
  weakens as the Network wakes, so the city keeps sending people out for what keeps it standing. Nobody says in
  public that the wall's foundations are older than the city.
- **The player** is one of the people who go outside (the bible's word is taken by other games; in the briefing they
  are addressed by their unit, **Orion** (proposed, from the kit they wear)). No chosen hero: someone who takes the
  job and finds out what the job really was.

## Who wants what
- **The Rampart Directorate**: the military that holds the wall. Every expedition must serve the city's survival.
  Its call sign on the radio is **Rampart**. It hides an expedition that reached a root before the city's official
  history begins.
- **The Farbound**: scavengers, guides, settlements outside. They think the city is a prison dressed as a refuge.
  Some have learned to live beside certain Altered.
- **The Pale Institute**: scientists who study the Network. They believe understanding it is the only way through.
  They have woken a small root once already.
- **The Still Choir**: people in the wilderness who think the Stillfall was a passage, not a disaster. Some hear the
  Network without instruments. Their sign in our game is a broken ring pierced by a spike.

## How it is told
Nothing is explained outright in the first campaign. Places, objects and conflicting accounts carry it. The three
things the player comes to see, in order: the world is not random; the monsters are connected to us; something is
listening. The question under all of it: what if they are not invading our home, but rebuilding it?
