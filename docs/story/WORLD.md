story bible (the Stillfall, the Pale Network, the Keystones, the Altered as changed people): none of that is true
in the game any more, and the old text is in git (before this date) if it is ever wanted. The names here are ours
and can be changed in one place; nothing gives a date or a length of time for the war, on the owner's word.

## The story
Humanity had spread through its own system: stations round Earth, cities on the Moon and Mars, outposts as far as
the outer moons. Then an empire came in from outside.

We met it at the edge of the system and lost. It came inward a world at a time, the outer moons, the belt, Mars,
the Moon, and we lost each one more slowly than the last, until the fight was in the sky over Earth and the ring of
stations round the planet was breaking.

That is when it turned, and nobody on Earth understood what they were seeing. The empire's own ships began firing on
each other. Destroyers rammed freighters. Landing forces on the ground turned their guns on the column next to
them. Within days most of the fleet was pulling out of the system, still fighting itself as it went. We hit what we
could reach as it left, and some of it never got away.

What stayed behind is still here: on Earth, in the wreck of the orbit and on every world they had taken from us.
They are not one army any more. Each species holds its own ground and means to have the whole system for its own
people. They can still reach space in what small craft they have; none of them has the means to build a ship that
could leave, and none of them wants to. They fight their neighbours for Sol, and that is the only reason we are
still alive.

We have spent the years since taking their machines apart, and we have come further in those years than in the two
centuries before. The game begins here, with people who carry that stolen technology going out to take our system
back, one held place at a time.

## The empire
- **The Vanthos Empire**. Many species under one throne, each with its own worlds, its own fleets and
  its own lords. They did not like each other. The emperor was the only thing they all obeyed.
- **What Earth does not know**: the emperor was murdered while the fleet was over Earth. The quarrel between the
  species had been growing for a generation; with the throne empty it became open war in an hour, in every fleet
  the empire had, ours included. Every species has called its ships home to fight for the throne and none will
  bend to another. Who killed the emperor is not known even inside the empire. This is the campaign's long secret.
- **What Earth fears**: that when one of them has won, it will come back.
- **Void crystal**: what the empire's machines are grown from: power, memory and armour in one material. Every
  species used it; none of them made it. Everything we have learned comes from cutting it open.

## Who holds what now
Each species that was left behind holds the ground it was standing on. They are as different from each other as
they are from us: some walk upright like people, some do not look like anything that should think. A place held by
one species has only that species in it, which is why Meridian has one enemy and Mars will have another.

- **Earth, the city of Meridian: the Sarketh**. Humanoid. See below: they are the game's first
  enemy and the only one built so far.
- **Everything else is still to be designed**, one species at a time, each with its own bodies, weapons, way of
  fighting and name. Working ideas only, to show how different they can be (none of this is decided):
  - the wreck of the ring: something that lives in vacuum and wears the hulks like shells;
  - the Moon: a burrowing, many-limbed species that never comes up in daylight;
  - Mars: a big four-legged species that fights from its own living mounts;
  - the outer moons: something that swims, under the ice.

## The Sarketh: the species on Meridian
One people, humanoid, built much like us. They are all the same size under what they wear: what makes one of them
small and quick and another a wall of metal is **what he has been issued**. The force on Meridian has been cut off
since the fleet left, so what it has is what it landed with, and that goes by rank. A foot soldier gets a light gun
and little else. The higher the command, the more armour and the bigger the gun, up to powered armour and, for the
one who commands the garrison, a walking war suit. Every one of them carries a gun.

- What they want: Sol, for the Sarketh. Every species that was left here wants the same for its own people, which
  is why they fight each other before they fight us.
- What this means for building them: ONE body, and armour and weapons in tiers on top of it, the way the player's
  outfit is made of pieces. The big ones are suits with one of them inside, not bigger creatures.
- What stays as it is in the game: every role, ability, number and behaviour. What changes: every body, every
  name, every voice (they are soldiers with a language, not husks), and what they carry and wear becomes their own
  make. One thing to decide when the scouts are built: today that role has claws and no gun; with a sidearm they
  would fire on the way in and still close to blade range.

## Humanity now
- **Shattered Orbit**: the broken ring of stations and the hulks of the fleet that died there, still overhead.
- **Nexus Prime**: the first city rebuilt, raised round a grounded Vanthos carrier whose heart still runs and
  powers the city. Shields, light guns and the first new ships of our own come out of it.
- **The Rampart Directorate**: the command that holds what has been retaken. Call sign **Rampart**.
- **The Institute**: the people who take the machines apart. They have given the city everything it has and they
  have made mistakes that cost districts.
- **The Farbound**: the people who never came back inside the cities.
- **Orion**: the program the player belongs to. Small teams with the best of the stolen technology, sent into the
  places still held to kill what holds them and bring back what works.

## What drives it
1. Why did they turn on each other?
2. Is one of them coming back, and can we be ready?
3. Who killed the emperor, and what happens to us when the war for the throne is over?

## What this replaced, and what it kept
Gone: the Stillfall, the Pale Network and its roots, the Keystones, the Quiet, the wall's failing field, the Still
Choir, the Altered as changed people, and every date. Kept: Nexus Prime, Meridian and every place name in it, the
Directorate and its call sign, the Institute, the Farbound, Orion.

## Where the game stands against it
- The operation's words (briefing, radio, objectives, end screen, the names over the enemies' heads and on the boss
  bar) follow this story since 2026-11-14.
- The enemies' BODIES do not yet: Meridian still has the first bodies (husks grown through with crystal). The
  Sarketh are being rebuilt as one humanoid people in `tools/blender/hero/sarketh.py`, one soldier first (a Veteran),
  shown to the owner before any other rank is built. Ids, classes and asset folders keep the first names
  (`remnant`, `stalker`, `veilborn`, `bulwark`, `bearer`, `colossus`): they are an API, not what the player reads.
- The light (energy) guns are the Sarketh's own technology (the owner, 2026-11-14), so their armour is made like
  those guns, and those guns fire plasma, not bullets (in the game since 2026-11-14: a bolt that is seen to fly,
  a splash where it lands; only the shots, the guns' models are as they were). Their red is cloth, and it carries
  their emblem: the helm with no face, seen from the front (`sarketh_surface.emblem`). The Void Requiem set's look
  is ANOTHER species' technology, to be used when that species is made. Open: Meridian Fall still rewards the
  Void Requiem set and the Void Sentinel armour, which no longer come from the species that holds Meridian.
- No other species exists. Each is designed when the owner asks for the place it holds.
- Nothing in the levels shows the war itself yet (the fallen station as a place, wrecks of the fleet).
