# Shattered Orbit (Unreal Engine 5.8)

Stages 2-4 of `docs/unreal/MIGRATION_PLAN.md`. Stage 2 (foundation): project, modules, player movement and
camera, input, graphics settings, a generated test range. Stage 3 (first playable combat): the Blender player body,
the kinetic assault rifle with its physical reload, the VANTHOS stalker with AI, GAS health / shield / damage,
death and respawn, a combat HUD. Stage 4 (graphics + feedback): tracers, muzzle flashes, sparks, dust, creature
blood, bullet holes, synthesised sound for everything, a post-process look and a benchmark. The browser build in the repository root stays the playable prototype until this
project overtakes it.

## Generate the content (editor Python scripts)

Everything in `Content/` is generated by scripts (the `.uasset`/`.umap` results are committed through LFS, so this is
only needed after changing a script or a Blender model). Order: Blender export, input, assets, map.

1. Export the GLBs from `assets/models` to `SourceArt/` (FBX + textures + json, not committed) and render the sounds
   (`tools/audio/sfx_synth.py`, needs numpy + scipy). Skeletal files are written in centimetres (Unreal's unit
   conversion scales skinned vertices but not the bind pose):

```bash
bash tools/blender/export_unreal.sh
```


```bash

```

```bash
"/c/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_5.8/Engine/Binaries/Win64/UnrealEditor-Cmd.exe" "C:/Dev/World-ex/unreal/ShatteredOrbit/ShatteredOrbit.uproject" -run=pythonscript -script="C:/Dev/World-ex/unreal/ShatteredOrbit/Content/Python/make_test_map.py" -unattended -nullrhi -nosplash
```

In the editor: Tools > Execute Python Script.

- `setup_input.py` → `/Game/Input/IA_*` (Move, Look, LookGamepad, Jump, Sprint, Crouch, ToggleView, Interact, Fire,
  Aim, Reload) and `IMC_Default` (keyboard/mouse + gamepad).
- `make_test_map.py` → `/Game/Maps/L_TestRange` (floor with a 1 m checker, walls, mantle ledges at 60/120/200/240 cm
  plus a 300 cm wall, ramps at 15/30/50 degrees, crates, sky atmosphere, clouds, sun, sky light, height fog, post
  process, player start) and the grey-box material `/Game/Materials/Dev/M_Greybox`; sets it as the default map.
  The grey-box blocks are the engine unit cube with our own procedural material: test geometry only. Stage 3 adds a
  nav mesh bounds volume (the nav mesh builds at runtime) and three stalker spawners 26-34 m out.
- `import_assets.py` → our own default textures and the master material `/Game/Materials/M_SOStandard` (glTF
  semantics: base colour x tint x vertex colour, metal/rough map G = roughness, B = metal, OpenGL normal maps flipped
  on import), then from `SourceArt`: `/Game/Characters/PlayerMale/SK_PlayerMale` (default tactical loadout, 10 clips
  as `Anims/A_PlayerMale_*`), `/Game/Characters/Stalker/SK_Stalker` (5 clips, hx_* flesh textures by material name),
  `/Game/Weapons/Rifle/SM_Rifle` + `SM_Rifle_mag` + `SM_Rifle_bolt` and `DA_Rifle` (USOWeaponData with the data.js
  numbers and the part pivots / muzzle / sight / foregrip from `gun_rifle.parts.json`), `/Game/Weapons/Arms/*`.
  Material instances are built from each GLB's `materials.json`; the FBX carry slot names only.
  Stage 4 adds the effect cards `/Game/FX/SM_FX_Quad|Streak|Star` (from `tools/blender/fx_kit.py`), the unlit effect
  material `M_SOFx` (colour + intensity per instance, procedural dot / streak shapes; `MI_FxGlow`, `MI_FxStreak`,
  `MI_FxFlash`, `MI_FxSmoke`), the procedural bullet-hole decal `M_SOBulletHole`, and the 26 sounds `/Game/Audio/S_*`.

## What to try in the editor

Open `ShatteredOrbit.uproject` (L_TestRange opens), press Play.

- **Move / look:** WASD + mouse, or left / right stick. Space / A jumps.
- **Sprint:** hold Shift (or tap it to latch until you stop pushing forward); L3 on the gamepad.
- **Crouch / slide:** C or Ctrl (B on the gamepad); tap toggles, hold crouches while held. Crouch while sprinting
  slides (~5 m, jump out of it to keep the speed).
- **Mantle:** walk or jump at a ledge while holding jump: the orange blocks (60-240 cm) are climbable, the grey
  300 cm wall is not.
- **Coyote time / jump buffer:** jump just after running off a ledge, or press jump just before landing.
- **Camera:** T (D-pad up) toggles third person; RMB / LT zooms (aim) with zoom-matched sensitivity.
- **Look:** mouse = 0.07 degrees per count at sensitivity 1 (`SOGameUserSettings.MouseSensitivity` scales it).
- **Shoot:** LMB / RT fires the rifle (600 rpm, real rounds with drop, 19 damage, x1.6 on the head). RMB / LT aims
  down the sights (1.35x zoom, 58 % move speed, tighter cone). Firing or aiming stops a sprint; after a sprint the
  rifle needs 0.2 s before it fires.
- **Reload:** R / X. Watch the mag rock forward off its lip and drop, the left hand fetch a new one from the pouch,
  hook and seat it; on an empty mag the hand then racks the bolt. Sprinting cancels the reload; pulling the trigger on
  an empty mag starts one.
- **Stalkers:** three spawn 26-34 m out. They wander, notice you by sight (or your gunfire), run you down along the
  nav mesh and lunge in with 18-damage strikes. 140 health; dead ones respawn after 8 s. Bars show over hurt ones.
- **Die / respawn:** your 50 shield takes damage first and refills 4 s after the last hit (25/s). At 0 health the
  camera pulls out to third person, the body plays its death clip and you redeploy at the player start after 4 s.
- **HUD:** crosshair sized by spread, hit markers (yellow = head, red = kill), health / shield, ammo, reload bar, a
  red (health) or blue (shield) flash when you are hit.
- **Effects:** every round draws a tracer from the muzzle to where it lands; hard surfaces throw sparks and dust and
  keep a bullet hole (64 at most); creatures bleed their own colour (stalker: violet). Muzzle flash with a short light.
- **Sound:** gunshot (your own in stereo, others positional with distance filtering), every reload step (catch, mag
  out, pouch, mag in, seat, bolt rack), dry fire, impacts, footsteps, stalker attack / hurt / death, your hurt grunt,
  shield break and recharge, hit / precision / kill ticks.
- **Interact** (E) only logs for now.
- **Graphics settings:** console `r.DynamicGlobalIlluminationMethod`, or in a Blueprint / Python get
  `SOGameUserSettings`, change e.g. `bLumen`, `AntiAliasing`, `ScreenPercentage` and call `ApplyGraphics`.
- **Multiplayer check:** Play > Number of Players 2, Net Mode "Play As Listen Server": sprint, slide and mantle on the
  client should not rubber-band.

## Layout

| Module | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ShatteredOrbit` (primary) | `ASOGameMode` (HUD, respawn after 4 s), `ASOPlayerController` (adds `IMC_Default`, `SOCaptureSeries`), `ASOPlayerState`, `USOGameInstance`, tests |
| `SOCore` | `LogShatteredOrbit`, `USOGameUserSettings` (look + graphics settings, `ApplyGraphics()`) |
| `SOCharacter` | `ASOCharacter` (weapon, ADS, recoil, body + clips), `USOCharacterMovementComponent` (sprint, ADS speed, coyote, slide, mantle, saved moves), `USOLookComponent`, `USOViewmodelComponent` (viewmodel.js port), `USOLocomotionAnimInstance` (native blend of the Blender clips, no anim blueprint) |
| `SOCombat` | GAS: `USOAttributeSet` (health, shield, incoming damage), `USOAbilitySystemComponent` (shield regen), `USOGameplayEffect_Damage`, `USOCombatStatics` (damage, factions, death + noise events); `ASOCombatCharacter` (base for player and creatures), `USOWeaponData`, `ASOWeapon` (server-authoritative trigger / ammo / reload), `USOGunRigComponent` (posable gun + arms), `SOReload` (reload.js keyframe port), `USOProjectileSubsystem` (rounds with drop on the SOBullet channel), `USOFxSubsystem` (instanced-card particles, decals, flash lights), `USOSoundLibrary` (named sounds, range presets) |
| `SOAI` | `ASOEnemy` (stalker), `ASOEnemyAIController` (sight / hearing / chase / melee), `ASOEnemySpawner` |
| `SOWorld` | `USOTimeOfDaySubsystem` skeleton (rotates a directional light tagged `Sun`) |
| `SOUI` | `ASOHUD` (canvas combat HUD, damage flash, hit sounds), `USOActivatableWidget` (CommonUI base) |

Assets come from Blender: `tools/blender/export_fbx.py -- in.glb out_dir [--single] [--split-parts] [--only=a,b]`
turns our GLBs into Unreal-ready FBX (units, Z up, no leaf bones, UCX_ collision, LOD groups, reload parts at their
pivots, materials.json + the embedded images). `tools/blender/export_unreal.sh` runs it for everything the project
uses.

## Known gaps (next stages)

- Effects are a C++ particle system over Blender cards rather than Niagara (Niagara systems cannot be authored
  headless); sounds are pre-rendered WAVs from `tools/audio/sfx_synth.py` rather than MetaSounds graphs. Both can be
  swapped for editor-authored assets later without touching gameplay code. No 2K/4K texture re-bakes yet.
- Stalker ranged bolts are not in yet.
- The stalker brain is C++; the StateTree version comes when the creature roster grows.
- Third person: the rifle grip follows `hand_R` and the barrel follows the aim; no left-hand IK or upper-body aim
  offset yet, so the arms do not wrap the gun.
- First-person arms are the browser build's static `viewmodel_arms.glb` (no fingers), posed by the reload timeline.
- Clients see the server's shots (no client-side prediction yet); hit markers only for the listen-server host.
