# Shattered Orbit (Unreal Engine 5.8)

Stages 2-5 of `docs/unreal/MIGRATION_PLAN.md`. Stage 2 (foundation): project, modules, player movement and
camera, input, graphics settings, a generated test range. Stage 3 (first playable combat): the Blender player body,
the kinetic assault rifle with its physical reload, the VANTHOS stalker with AI, GAS health / shield / damage,
death and respawn, a combat HUD. Stage 4 (graphics + feedback): tracers, muzzle flashes, sparks, dust, creature
blood, bullet holes, synthesised sound for everything, a post-process look and a benchmark. Stage 5 (looter systems):
all 22 weapon archetypes of the browser build (kinetic, energy, horror) with their models, optics, firing (pellets,
bursts, energy bolts, rockets, acid / bile / brood goo with splash and acid over time) and every reload choreography;
rolled copies (rarity, perks, stat rolls, names), an inventory that is saved, three equip slots, loot cores and ammo
from kills, the VANTHOS reliquary reward pool and an inventory screen. The browser build in the repository root stays the playable prototype until this
project overtakes it.

## Generate the content (editor Python scripts)

Everything in `Content/` is generated by scripts (the `.uasset`/`.umap` results are committed through LFS, so this is
only needed after changing a script or a Blender model). Order: Blender export, input, assets, map.

0. Game data: `node tools/unreal/export_game_data.mjs` writes `Data/game_data.json` (committed) from

   (`tools/audio/sfx_synth.py`, needs numpy + scipy). Skeletal files are written in centimetres (Unreal's unit
   conversion scales skinned vertices but not the bind pose):

- `setup_input.py` → `/Game/Input/IA_*` (Move, Look, LookGamepad, Jump, Sprint, Crouch, ToggleView, Interact, Fire,

  nav mesh bounds volume (the nav mesh builds at runtime) and three stalker spawners 26-34 m out.
- `import_assets.py` → our own default textures and the master material `/Game/Materials/M_SOStandard` (glTF

  on import), then from `SourceArt`: the players (below), `/Game/Characters/Stalker/SK_Stalker` (5 clips, hx_* flesh textures by material name),
  `/Game/Weapons/Rifle/SM_Rifle` + `SM_Rifle_mag` + `SM_Rifle_bolt` and `DA_Rifle` (USOWeaponData with the data.js
  numbers and the part pivots / muzzle / sight / foregrip from `gun_rifle.parts.json`) and `/Game/Weapons/Arms/*`.
  `SO_IMPORT_ONLY=player,weapons,...` re-imports just those steps (player, stalker, weapons, fx, audio).
  Players (modular, both bodies): `export_fbx.py --per-item --finger-rig` writes the body (every skin region its own
  `skin__<region>` section) and every wardrobe piece as its own skeletal FBX on one skeleton (with finger bones), plus
  the first-person cut of each (`--arms-only`). They become `/Game/Characters/Player<Male|Female>/SK_Player<Sex>` (body,
  clips as `Anims/A_Player<Sex>_*`, physics), `SK_Player<Sex>Arms`, `Wardrobe/SK_<Sex>_<slot>_<item>`,
  `Arms/SK_<Sex>Arms_<slot>_<item>`, `Icons/T_Icon_<slot>_<item>` and `DA_Wardrobe` (USOWardrobeData: labels, the
  regions each piece hides, locked underwear, armour ratings from data.js `armorStats`, defaults). Gun icons
  (`tools/blender/icons.py`, Cycles renders of our models) land as `/Game/Weapons/<Gun>/T_Icon_<Gun>`.
  Stage 5: every gun of `Data/game_data.json` becomes `/Game/Weapons/<Gun>/DA_<Gun>` (stats, slot, projectile kind,
  sockets, rail, optics it takes) with `SM_<Gun>` + its reload parts (shared textures in `/Game/Weapons/Textures`), the
  optics `/Game/Weapons/Attach/SM_ATT_*` (eye points from `gun_attach.glb`), `/Game/Data/DA_LootTables` (rarities,
  perks, sights, finishes, name pools, the archetype list) and the loot props `/Game/Props/Loot/SM_PR_core|ammo`,
  `/Game/Props/Reliquary/SM_VX_reliquary`. `setup_input.py` adds IA_Weapon1-3, IA_Next/PrevWeapon, IA_Inventory;
  `make_test_map.py` places a reliquary on the range.
  Material instances are built from each GLB's `materials.json`; the FBX carry slot names only.
  Stage 4 adds the effect cards `/Game/FX/SM_FX_Quad|Streak|Star` (from `tools/blender/fx_kit.py`), the unlit effect
  material `M_SOFx` (colour + intensity per instance, procedural dot / streak shapes; `MI_FxGlow`, `MI_FxStreak`,
  `MI_FxFlash`, `MI_FxSmoke`), the procedural bullet-hole decal `M_SOBulletHole`, and the 26 sounds `/Game/Audio/S_*`.
  Gun wear: `tools/blender/wear_textures.py` bakes `assets/textures/wear_mask_c.jpg` (R scratches, G grime, B chips);
  the guns' `_wear` vertex attribute (edges / exposed parts) reaches the FBX as vertex colour alpha (`1 - wear`), and
  the master material's wear inputs (`WearTex`, `WearTiling`, `EdgeWear`, `Scratches`, `Grime`, `WearMetal`; all 0 by
  default) are set per gun material from `GUN_WEAR` in `import_assets.py`: worn steel shows bright metal on the edges,
  polymer and wood scuff and gather grime. `SO_IMPORT_ONLY=ui` adds `M_UI_Stage` (the loadout stage picture);
  `nexus` imports the Nexus Prime kit (`tools/blender/nexus.py` + `nexus_textures.py`) as `/Game/Environments/Nexus/SM_NX_*`.

### Nexus Prime, the big city (`L_Nexus`)

8.3 x 5.3 km inside its wall, after the owner's master plan (`art/reference/nexus_prime_plan.png`):
1. `python tools/city/nexus_plan.py` (shapely) traces the plan into `Data/nexus_plan.json`: wall and gates, the noded
   road network (highway / ring / primary / secondary / access with widths, 2.4k nodes, 459 km), blocks, ~12.7k lots
   with a height each, districts, landmarks, the metro loop (16 m) with 8 hubs and two rail lines (9 m) with stations.
2. `blender --background --python tools/blender/nexus_city_textures.py` paints the ground sets (`nc_*`: asphalt,
   pavers, plaza, kerb, lot, grass, roof, wall) and the facades (`ncf_*`, one bay x one floor; mask R = glass).
3. `blender --background --python tools/blender/nexus_city.py` writes the kit `assets/models/nexus_city_kit.glb`
   (`NCB_<shape>_<style>` buildings on a unit footprint, `NCK_*` lamp, station, roof clutter) and bakes the plan into
   1 km tiles (`SourceArt/NexusCity/src/nexus_city_tiles.glb`: asphalt, raised blocks with kerbs, landmark grounds,
   lane paint with crossings and stop lines, rail / metro viaducts on piers outside the roads, the wall with towers,
   gates and rail portals; `NCL_land` = country and mountains outside), plus `Data/nexus_city.json` (tiles,
   designs per district, roofs, stations, lane layout per road class, tilings). `export_unreal.sh` exports both.
4. `SO_IMPORT_ONLY=nexuscity` imports `/Game/Environments/NexusCity` (Nanite, complex collision) with
   `M_SOGround` (textures projected in world metres) and `M_SOFacade` (UV0 / UV1 facade coordinates x the instance's
   custom data = real bays and floors on any lot; hashed lit windows scaled by `Night`).
5. `make_nexus_map.py` makes `/Game/Maps/L_Nexus`: one `ASONexusCity` (it builds tiles, a building per lot sized to the
   lot's rectangle, roof clutter, kerb lamps, stations and the scaled Nexus landmarks at BeginPlay / in the editor,
   nothing saved in the level; keeps `RoadNodes`, `RoadEdges` with lanes per direction and `Stations` for the coming
   traffic, people and trains), sky, haze, the map (gates, districts, Spire, metro hubs, the shuttle back) and the
   start on the Spire plaza. `SOCaptureSeries 1 4` in `L_Nexus` takes street, gate, aerial and night pictures.

## What to try in the editor

Open `ShatteredOrbit.uproject` (L_TestRange opens), press Play.

- **Move / look:** WASD + mouse, or left / right stick. Space / A jumps.
- **Sprint:** hold Shift (or tap it to latch until you stop pushing forward); L3 on the gamepad.
- **Crouch / slide:** C or Ctrl (B on the gamepad); tap toggles, hold crouches while held. Crouch while sprinting
  slides (~5 m, jump out of it to keep the speed).
- **Mantle:** walk or jump at a ledge while holding jump: the orange blocks (60-240 cm) are climbable, the grey
  300 cm wall is not.
- **Coyote time / jump buffer:** jump just after running off a ledge, or press jump just before landing.
- **Fluid movement.** The approach follows Unreal's own sample characters, all native C++ with no anim blueprint.
  - Orientation warping: a strafe turns the legs toward the movement while the chest stays on the aim; a backpedal
    plays the cycle in reverse.
  - Leans into acceleration, landing compression, and two-bone foot IK that plants the feet on steps and slopes.
  - Mantles keep 80 % of the approach speed (a sprint vault flows on) and run quicker when fast.
  - Slides keep going down slopes.
  - First-person camera feel (local player only):
    - steps up and down stairs glide instead of popping;
    - the view dips with landings on a spring;
    - head bob in step with the footsteps (`HeadBobScale`);
    - a small tilt into slides and strafes;
    - a wider field of view while sprinting or sliding.
  - The gun trails strafes and landings, tucks into a slide and drops away during a mantle.
  - First-person reloads lift the gun about 8 cm and cant it about 24° (`ReloadLift`, `ReloadTilt` on the viewmodel), so
    the magazine well and the hands stay in view.
- **Camera:** T (D-pad up) toggles third person; RMB / LT zooms (aim) with zoom-matched sensitivity. Third-person
  shots go where the crosshair is (the round flies from the eyes to what the shoulder camera's centre ray hits).
- **Holding the gun:** the first-person arms are your own body's arms (same sleeves and gloves as in third person),
  and both bodies reach for the gun with arm IK: firing hand on the grip, support hand under the handguard. In first
  person aiming puts the iron sights on the crosshair (the rounds land on the front post, also while firing: the
  recoil kicks the view and the gun together). In third person the torso follows your pitch, blades and leans in, and
  aiming raises the rifle to the cheek.
- **Look:** mouse = 0.07 degrees per count at sensitivity 1 (`SOGameUserSettings.MouseSensitivity` scales it).
- **Shoot:** LMB / RT fires the rifle (600 rpm, real rounds with drop, 19 damage, x1.6 on the head). RMB / LT aims
  down the sights (1.35x zoom, 58 % move speed, tighter cone). Firing or aiming stops a sprint; after a sprint the
  rifle needs 0.2 s before it fires.
- **Weapons:** 1 / 2 / 3 (or the mouse wheel, D-pad left / right) switch between the Primary, Special and Heavy
  slots. A new profile starts with the browser build's starters (auto rifle, hand cannon, rocket launcher) plus an
  armoury copy of every kinetic and horror gun; the assault rifle is in hand. Every gun has one name (data.js
  `EXOTIC_NAMES`); copies differ by small stat rolls (a few percent) and by their perks, and wear their rolled optic
  (reflex, holo, 2x / 4x / 8x scopes; fully aimed through magnified glass the scope picture replaces the gun). Both
  hands grip the gun: the fingers close round the pistol grip (index on the trigger), the handguard, magazines and
  rounds. The horror guns leave a poison pool for 5 s where they land.
  - Guns are real size (`weapons.GUN_SCALE`; table in `tools/blender/proportions.md`).
  - With a rail optic mounted, the gun's iron sights (the `irons` part) fold away.
  - Aiming puts the optic's eye point at the eye. A red dot or holographic window then frames about a quarter of
    the view, with only a light zoom (1.2× / 1.25×).
  - The crosshair fades out over the first third of aiming.
- **Loadout (inventory):** I / Tab (View on the gamepad). Weapons and the outer layers (coat, rig) on the left,
  armour (head, chest, hands, legs, feet) on the right, the player in the middle as they stand (drag to turn; the
  body button switches male / female). Click a slot: its grid of everything owned (icons in rarity colours, NEW and
  locked marks) and the hovered item's details (stats against the equipped one, perks, armour). Equip / wear (E, or
  click the selected item again), Lock (L), Dismantle (X twice, +alloy), Esc backs out. Armour pieces are the
  players' wardrobe pieces (the Orion set from the owner's concept sheet is worn by default). Saved in
  `Saved/SaveGames/ShatteredOrbit.sav` (delete it for a fresh profile).
- **Loot:** kills pay credits and xp; 35 % leave ammo, 15 % a loot core glowing in its rarity colour (a light shaft
  from Rare up). Walk over it: the core becomes a rolled weapon around your power, announced in the feed on the right.
- **Reliquary:** the gold box near the start. Interact (E / Y) when the prompt shows: 2-3 weapons from the VANTHOS
  reward pool (Uncommon to Exotic) plus credits, alloy and xp. On the range it reseals after 30 s.
- **Reload:** R / X. Watch the mag rock forward off its lip and drop, the left hand fetch a new one from the pouch,
  hook and seat it; on an empty mag the hand comes over the top, palm down, hooks the charging handle and rides it
  back. Sprinting cancels the reload; pulling the trigger on
  an empty mag starts one.
- **Stalkers:** three spawn 26-34 m out. They wander, notice you by sight (or your gunfire), run you down along the
  nav mesh and lunge in with 18-damage strikes. 140 health; dead ones respawn after 8 s. Bars show over hurt ones.
- **Die / respawn:** your 50 shield takes damage first and refills 4 s after the last hit (25/s). At 0 health the
  camera pulls out to third person, the body plays its death clip and you redeploy at the player start after 4 s.
- **HUD:** crosshair sized by spread, hit markers (yellow = head, red = kill), health / shield, ammo, reload bar, a
  red (health) or blue (shield) flash when you are hit.
- **Effects:** every round draws a tracer from the muzzle to where it lands; hard surfaces throw sparks and dust and
  keep a bullet hole (64 at most); creatures bleed their own colour (stalker: violet). Muzzle flash with a short light.
- **Sound:** gunshot (your own in stereo, others positional with distance filtering), every reload step (catch, mag
  out, pouch, mag in, seat, bolt rack), dry fire, impacts, footsteps, stalker attack / hurt / death, your hurt grunt,
  shield break and recharge, hit / precision / kill ticks.
- **Map and quick travel:** the mini-map (top left, north up, 300 m round you or the whole area if it is smaller)
  shows the important places in their kind's colour. M (D-pad down) opens the map screen: hover a place for its
  name, click a fast-travel point to go there, an area gate or an area on the right (Nexus Prime, Proving Range) to
  travel. Each level carries an `ASOMapInfo` (area name, half size; it captures the top-down picture) and
  `ASOMapPoint`s (kind, label, fast travel, travel level).
- **Death:** the gun leaves the hands and falls with the body (physics copy of the third-person gun, 30 s).
- **Interact** (E) only logs for now.
- **Graphics settings:** console `r.DynamicGlobalIlluminationMethod`, or in a Blueprint / Python get
  `SOGameUserSettings`, change e.g. `bLumen`, `AntiAliasing`, `ScreenPercentage` and call `ApplyGraphics`.
- **Multiplayer check:** Play > Number of Players 2, Net Mode "Play As Listen Server": sprint, slide and mantle on the
  client should not rubber-band.

Assets come from Blender: `tools/blender/export_fbx.py -- in.glb out_dir [--single] [--split-parts] [--only=a,b] [--arms-only] [--per-item] [--finger-rig=male|female]`

uses.

## Known gaps (next stages)

- Effects are a C++ particle system over Blender cards rather than Niagara (Niagara systems cannot be authored
  headless); sounds are pre-rendered WAVs from `tools/audio/sfx_synth.py` rather than MetaSounds graphs. Both can be
  swapped for editor-authored assets later without touching gameplay code. No 2K/4K texture re-bakes yet.
- Stalker ranged bolts are not in yet.
- The stalker brain is C++; the StateTree version comes when the creature roster grows.
- Finger poses are presets per grip (trigger, wrap, pinch, flat), not traced against each gun's shape; the firing
  hand uses one grip shape for every gun (the origin is always the top of the pistol grip).
- The players are procedural (signed-distance sculpting in Blender): far better than before, still short of
  hand-sculpted / scanned AAA faces. Armour has no perks or loot drops yet (every piece is owned from the start).
- The brood launcher's swarm is an area of chewing damage (the Writhe mites themselves come with the enemies of stage
  7). Shells / casings ejected from the guns are not simulated yet (spent mags, speedloaders and husks drop).
- The inventory profile is local (the listen-server host's save); clients get a fresh profile until the profile
  service. No vendors / shader (finish) changes yet; the Gunsmith, armour and the operation level are later stages.
- Clients see the server's shots (no client-side prediction yet); hit markers only for the listen-server host.
